# The Bog art

PixelLab (subscription), picked by Terrence on 2026-11-08. Prompts, seeds, raw takes and
mocks are in the project folder at sum-dungeon/art-bog/. Grid and look rules:
docs/art-style-guide.md.

Not drawn by any screen yet: the Bog levels are milestone 6 of docs/difficulty-plan.md.
Characters are 1 art px = 1 unit; bog-bg (seamless, tile it along the crossing), fight-bg
and treasure-room are 320x180 drawn at 3x. Stones (tussock, log, peat, two each) are
136x76 like the Lava rocks, centered on the hop line. Wisp King and Queen are the miniboss
pair; Jenny Greenteeth + jenny-arm are the boss, laid out like the Skeleton Dragon.
