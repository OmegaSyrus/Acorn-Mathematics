# Space art

PixelLab (subscription, pixflux), 2026-11-12. Terrence asked for the remaining worlds to be
finished, so the takes were picked without a pick round; prompts, seeds, every raw take and
the scripts are in the project folder at sum-dungeon/art-space/. Grid and look rules:
docs/art-style-guide.md. Palette: palette-space-24.hex (deep indigo, star gold, moon cream).

Characters are 1 art px = 1 unit and face right in the file (the game mirrors them to face
the hero): star-squirrel, moon-hobgoblin, fenrir-pup, ratatoskr (miniboss). Fenrir is the
boss: fenrir.png (head, top right past the board) and fenrir-paw.png (a quarter turn of the
take, so it reaches in from the right). space-bg (seamless, tiled along the crossing),
fight-bg and treasure-room are 320x180 drawn at 3x. fight-bg has a hand-painted branch
floor at the fighters' feet (fight_floor.py). stone-0..2 are the star stones: the top of
the sprite is where the hero stands, and a wrong one falls away into the dark.
