# Mushroom Forest art

PixelLab (subscription), made 2026-11-12 and picked by Claude from two takes each (Terrence:
"finish the remaining worlds and bosses"). Prompts, seeds and raw takes are in the project
folder at sum-dungeon/art-mushroom-forest/. Grid and look rules: docs/art-style-guide.md.

Characters are 1 art px = 1 unit: pixie, hobgoblin (acorn shield), boggart, the Hedley Kow
(miniboss). The Green Man + branch-arm are the boss, laid out like the Skeleton Dragon.
cap-rose, cap-brown and cap-red are the path: giant mushrooms side-on, 128 tall, the hero
stands on the cap and the stem runs down to the leaf litter. forest-bg (seamless, tiled
along the crossing), fight-bg and treasure-room are 320x180 drawn at 3x. leaf.png is the
leaves currency icon (not used by a screen yet).
