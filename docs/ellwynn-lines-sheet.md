# Ellwynn lines: situation sheet for Terrence and Rachel (2026-11-06)

One point per moment where Ellwynn can speak. Each point says when it happens,
what the child sees and just did, what the line is for, how long it can be,
and how many versions are worth writing. Write a line in the **Your line**
space and it replaces the placeholder for that point. The plan is
`docs/ellwynn-intro.md`.

Until a point has its own line, the game plays one of the three placeholders
at random: **"Yay!"**, **"Woo-hoo!"** or **"Let's go!"**.

**Rules for every line** (from the character bible): her math is always right,
she never gives the answer, she is warm, curious, a little silly and graceful.
Failing is real (the hero goes back to the start), so she can be kind about it
but should not pretend it didn't happen. The child reads 3 to 5 letter sight
words, so the voice carries the meaning; the small on-screen words already in
the game stay as they are.

**Length guide:** "short" is 1 to 2 seconds (2 to 4 words), "medium" is 3 to 5
seconds (one sentence), "long" is up to about 8 seconds (two sentences).
Points that happen many times need several versions so she doesn't repeat
herself.
