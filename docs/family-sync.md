# The parent app link, for grown-ups

Acorn Mathematics plays fully on its own: no account, no internet. A
grown-up can link the tablet to the family's parent app to see play
records on their phone, keep saves safe across tablets, and set a daily
time and a bedtime. Everything about the link sits behind the parent PIN.

## Linking the tablet

1. Main menu, **Grown-ups** (the parent PIN; you make one the first time),
   then **Family**.
2. **Show a pairing code.** A code like `K7Q-42M` appears. On your phone,
   sign in to the parent app, choose Add a tablet and type the code (or open
   the link under it). The code lasts 10 minutes.
3. Once you approve it, the tablet shows its name and the family's children.
4. Tap each player on the tablet and pick who they are: one of your
   children, **New child** (the parent app adds them with the player's name
   and picture), or **Not linked**. Guest is never listed and never sent.
5. Turn on **Send play records and save backups to the parent app**. It is
   off until you turn it on; while it is off nothing about play leaves the
   tablet (only who is who, so the parent app can show the players).

## What is sent, once sending is on

- Play records: each question answered, right or wrong, and run events
  (a level started, a heart lost, a level cleared), for linked and unlinked
  players. A Guest's play is never sent.
- A backup of each linked child's save (stickers, marks, gems, shop items).
- Sending happens in the background when the game starts, when it comes
  back from the background, after a level, and every few minutes. With no
  internet, records wait on the tablet (it keeps them until they are sent)
  and go later. The game never waits for the network.

## Saves on more than one tablet

When a player is linked to a child who already plays on another tablet, the
game merges that child's backups into this tablet's save: nothing earned on
either tablet is lost (marks, stars, stickers and items are combined, gems
are counted per tablet). **Restore from parent app** does it again on
demand. The save from just before a restore is kept on the tablet.

## Daily time and bedtime

Set them per child in the parent app:

- **Minutes a day** (0 to 240, 0 means no limit): minutes played today on
  this tablet, counted by the tablet's own clock, starting again at midnight.
- **Bedtime** (a time, or none): no play from then until 5 in the morning.

When the time is up, the game finishes the part being played (never in the
middle of a question), saves everything and shows **"Time to rest! Come back
tomorrow."** (or "It's bedtime!") with Ellwynn. A grown-up can tap
**Grown-ups** and type the PIN to let that player go on until the next
morning. The tablet remembers the settings, so they work offline too. They
apply only to players linked to a child; a Guest or an unlinked player has
no limits.

## Unlinking

**Unlink this tablet** on the Family screen forgets the link on the tablet.
Remove the tablet in the parent app too; a tablet removed there stops
sending by itself and plays on unlinked. Play records already on the
tablet stay on the tablet.
